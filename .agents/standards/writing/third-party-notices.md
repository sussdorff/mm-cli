## doc-modes.md (Diátaxis material)

The mode taxonomy and per-mode guidance in `doc-modes.md` adapt the Diátaxis
documentation framework by Daniele Procida (https://diataxis.fr), licensed
under CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
Modifications: condensed to a summary table and short capsules, combined with
instruction-sentence guidance from other sources. As a derivative of BY-SA
material, `doc-modes.md` is itself distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

## doc-modes.md (Google material)

The instruction-sentence guidance adapts the Google developer documentation
style guide (https://developers.google.com/style), licensed under CC BY 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Modifications: condensed to
the rules relevant for procedures.

## unambiguous-english.md (Kohl)

The rules restate principles from John R. Kohl, *The Global English Style
Guide: Writing Clear, Translatable Documentation for a Global Market* (SAS
Press, 2008), as facts in original wording. No text from the book is
reproduced.
